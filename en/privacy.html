First Hello — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Updated policy effective: October 16, 2026 (announced in app version 1.5.8) · Previous update: September 28, 2026 (effective September 30, 2026; announced in app version 1.5.4)

First Hello ("the app") is made by Minkeon Kim, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We keep the personal information you share with the app to a minimum and handle it with care. This policy applies to the English (US) edition of the app, published on the App Store as "First Hello: New Dad Checklist".

  • We do not collect advertising identifiers, location data, or your contacts.
  • The app contains no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Signing in never creates a new account. It uses the same information as linking an account.
  • US setup: If you choose US content and answer the health insurance type (employer, Marketplace, Medicaid, or not sure) and state questions, we save your answers to your account and sync them so deadline calculations carry over to a new device.

We do not store your account identifier, device information, email address, or any time more precise than the day, and we do not store the counts with your account. When the app sends a count, our servers may briefly keep a request log (such as the IP address).
  • Not sent to our servers: To show which account the records on your device belong to, the app keeps a partly hidden version of the linked account's email address (for example, ab***@gmail.com) and the sign-in method on your device, and shows them on the lock screen. This is not sent to our servers (it may be included in your device backups).

Dad space (posts by users)

  • Dad space is where dads share, in writing, what has stayed with them while caring for their baby. Right now only the Korean board is open; if the app language is English, the tab says it is not open yet.
  • You can read posts without linking an account. To post, react, report, or block, you need to link a Google or Apple account (the email address and social account identifier in the table above).
  • What other users see: the text of posts we have reviewed, the nickname shown with each post, and the date it was posted. The nickname is only what you type; the app does not fill it in from the baby's name you added on Home. For the block feature, the app also receives a random number that tells authors apart (not your account identifier; the same author's posts share the same number) and the exact time each post went up, but the screen shows only the date and never the number.
  • What stays private: your account details (email address and account identifier), the baby information you added on Home (name, date of birth, photo), and your progress. We do not show other users who reacted to, reported, or blocked a post, or how many reactions a post has, and we do not tell authors who reported them.
  • Review before posts appear: We read and review every post, and every edited post, before other users can see it. Until then, only you can see it in the app. Posts that break the ground rules are not shown or are removed from public view, and an author who repeatedly breaks them may be suspended. While suspended, that author's posts are hidden from other users and they cannot post, edit, or react (they can still delete their own posts, report, and block).
  • Posts are stored with your account, so when we review posts and handle reports we can see which account a post came from. We do not share account details or the reasons for our actions with other users.
  • Other users can read and copy posts once they are public. Please do not include details that could identify you, your child, your family, or anyone else. Please also do not post sensitive details, such as health information.
  • The ground rules and how we moderate Dad space are on our community guidelines page.
  • This information is stored with the same service provider and in the same place as described in Section 2. Nothing from Dad space is kept separately on your device.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties, except where the law requires us to. Posts and nicknames that you choose to publish in Dad space are shown to other users of the app after we review them.

4. How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

  • We keep your data until you delete it.
  • To delete your data, open the app and go to Settings → Delete account and data. The app deletes the current account's records (account, progress, baby's name, date of birth, and photo) from our servers and clears the app's data on your device. You do not need to contact us, and you can do this at any time. Previous accounts and copies in operational logs and backups are covered by the notes below.
  • You can also email us to ask for deletion, and we will take care of it for you.
  • When you sign in with an existing account on a new device, the anonymous account that was created automatically when you first opened the app on that device is deleted from our servers. If that anonymous account has records made on another device, the app tells you first, and if you choose to continue, those records are deleted too. If the deletion fails, an account with no records may remain on our servers.
  • If you try to sign in with an existing account that has no records, the sign-in does not go ahead and that empty account is deleted from our servers. If that account shows signs of recent use on another device, we keep it instead, and it may also remain if the deletion fails.
  • If you choose "Erase this device's records and start over" or "Continue with this device's records" on the app's record-protection (lock) screen, or start over after reinstalling the app, the server records of your previous account (progress, baby information, and photo) may not be deleted automatically.
    • If the previous account was anonymous: email us the text you copied with "Copy support info" — on the lock screen before tapping the button, or in Settings before deleting the app — and we will delete them after checking. Without that text, we cannot identify the previous account.
    • If the previous account was linked to Google or Apple: if you can sign in with that account on another device (one with no records), sign in and delete your account in the app. If you have no such device or cannot sign in, email us from the linked email address. We confirm it is you by checking that the sending address matches the email linked to that account, and then delete the account (its Dad space posts and other records are deleted too, just as when you delete your account in the app). If you linked with Apple's Hide My Email, so the linked email is an Apple relay address, we send a confirmation email to that relay address and confirm it is you when you reply to it.
  • Sign-in and record-recovery usage counts are statistics that are not linked to any account, so we do not set a retention period for them.
  • We do not delete accounts left behind as described above automatically; if you ask, we confirm it is you as described above and then delete them.
  • When you delete a post in Dad space, it is deleted from our servers, along with the reactions, reports, and moderation records for that post. We do not keep a copy of the deleted text or nickname. However, if another user blocked the author, the nickname saved in that user's block list stays until they unblock the author or either account is deleted. Posts removed from public view are kept, visible only to you, until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account and data in the app, your Dad space posts, reactions, reports, blocks, anti-abuse records, and any suspension are deleted too. This also deletes other users' reactions to and reports on your posts, and other users' blocks of you.
  • Reports are deleted together with the reported post, or with the reporter's or the author's account. We do not keep separate copies.
  • The times of your posts, edits, and reports stay after you delete a post and are deleted with your account (times older than a day are cleared the next time you use that feature). Posting receipts also stay after you delete a post, so that a delayed request cannot put a deleted post back up, and we keep them until you delete your account.
  • The automatic deletions during sign-in described above never delete an account that has Dad space records.
  • If you leave your previous account — for example, by reinstalling the app and starting over — the posts you made with that account stay public. If you can sign back in to that account, you can delete them in the app. If you cannot sign in (for example, the account has Dad space records but no baby records), email us from the linked email address (if you used Apple's Hide My Email, we confirm with a confirmation email as described above), and after checking we will remove those posts from public view.
  • Copies in operational logs and database backups (including deleted Dad space posts) may remain for their retention periods.

5. Your choices and requests

  • You can view your information at any time (it is shown inside the app), change it (the date of birth and photo), and delete it (Settings → Delete account and data).
  • You can also email us to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will confirm that the request came from you and respond without undue delay. There is no fee.
  • Anonymous accounts cannot be verified by email, so when you contact us we identify your account using the text from "Copy support info" in the app. It contains: the date it was copied (UTC), app version, platform (iOS or Android), country, account type, the sign-in method you linked, the account-switch stage and record-protection state, the first 8 characters of the current account identifier, the first 8 characters of the identifier of the account that owns the records, (if any) the first 8 characters of the identifiers of earlier owners of this device's records, and (when copied from a notice about a kept account) the first 8 characters of that account's identifier and its sign-in method. It does not include your baby's information, your email address or any full identifier. We use it only to answer your request, and we delete support emails one year after we finish handling them.
  • In Dad space, you can view, edit (edited posts are reviewed again before they appear), or delete your posts, and unblock authors. While only the Korean board is open, these screens are available when the app language is Korean; you can also email us.
  • Given the app's current size, we do not believe we meet the thresholds that would make state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) apply to us. Regardless, we offer the same ability to access and delete your data to every user, wherever you live.

6. Security

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Photos and other files are kept in private, non-public storage.
  • Row-level access controls (RLS) on the database block any account other than yours from reading your data.
  • If a security incident affects your data, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

7. Children

First Hello is made for adult parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The baby's date of birth and photo are entered by the parent, for the parent's own use in caring for their child, and the parent can delete them at any time.

8. Notifications

Deadline reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers to make them work. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Dad space — Community guidelines

Dad space ("아빠의 마음" in Korean) is the board in First Hello where dads share, in writing, what has stayed with them while caring for their baby. This page sets out the ground rules people agree to before posting, and how we moderate the board. Only the Korean board is open for now: if the app language is English, the Dad space tab says it isn't open yet. To see the Korean board, set Settings → App language to 한국어.

Ground rules

Listen without judging or lecturing. Do not post details that identify other people or children, abuse, hate, harassment, violent or sexual content, ads, or spam. You can report posts and block authors. Posts that break these rules may be withheld or removed.

This is the app's own wording (rules version 2026-09-25-v1). On the Korean board, the same rules appear in Korean:

서로의 경험을 평가하거나 훈계하지 않아요. 타인·아이를 알아볼 수 있는 개인정보, 비난·혐오·괴롭힘, 폭력·성적 콘텐츠, 광고·스팸은 올리지 않아요. 불편한 글은 신고하거나 작성자를 차단할 수 있어요. 약속을 어긴 글은 공개되지 않거나 삭제될 수 있어요.

Before a post can be sent, the author must check "I agree to follow these rules" ("이 약속을 지키며 글을 나눌게요"). The rules can be opened again at any time from the More options menu at the top right of Dad space → "A few ground rules" ("서로를 위한 약속").

How we moderate

Review before posts appear
We read and review every post, and every edited post, before other users can see it. Until then, only the author can see it. Posts usually appear within a day.
Reporting
On any post, More options (⋯) → "Report" ("신고하기") lets you pick a reason: abuse, hate, or harassment; personal information exposed; ads or spam; or other inappropriate content. We check pending posts and reports twice a day. We never tell authors who reported them.
Blocking
On any post, More options (⋯) → "Block this author" ("이 작성자 차단") hides that author's posts from you. You can unblock them under More options at the top right → "Blocked authors" ("차단한 작성자").
Posts that break the rules
We do not publish them, or we remove them from public view. A post removed from public view stays visible only to its author, and is deleted when the author deletes it or deletes their account.
Suspending authors
An author who repeatedly breaks the rules may be suspended. While suspended, their posts are hidden from other users and they cannot post, edit, or react. They can still delete their own posts, report, and block.
Accounts and what is public
Anyone can read without an account. To post, react, report, or block, a Google or Apple account must be linked.
  • Other users see: posts we have reviewed, the nickname shown with each post, and the date it was posted.
  • Other users never see: account details (such as the email address), the baby information added on Home (name, date of birth, photo), who reacted to, reported, or blocked a post, or how many reactions a post has.
  • Posts are text only. There are no photo uploads, comments, or messages.
Please do not include details that could identify you, your child, your family, or anyone else, or sensitive details such as health information. How we handle this information is described in our Privacy Policy.

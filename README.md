## How the app works

Enter one date — the due date or the birth date. The app lays out what a dad should do next, stage by stage (pregnancy → hospital → paperwork → home → health → the months ahead), and shows exactly one task at a time. Deadlines that cost money or rights when missed are tracked as D-day countdowns with escalating reminders. Content is country-specific (48 tasks in Korea, 37 in Japan, 35 in the United States) and is updated from the server when rules change, without an app update.

Facts come from official sources only (정부24 · 복지로 · 国民健康保険 / こども家庭庁 · 厚生労働省 / HealthCare.gov · U.S. Department of Labor · SSA) and are re-verified on a schedule. The app is a checklist, not legal, medical, or tax advice.

## Naming

To keep search results distinct from a Korean TV drama and books of the same title, please refer to the app as **"아빠가처음이라 앱"**, **"First Hello: New Dad Checklist"**, or **"パパ、はじめまして アプリ"**.

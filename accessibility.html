English — First Hello

This matches the Accessibility section on the App Store. Only features we have checked are listed.

iPhone · iPad

  • Dark Interface — follows your system setting, or choose Dark in Settings → Appearance.
  • Differentiate Without Color Alone — deadline warnings, eligibility badges and completed states also use text (e.g. "7 days left", "Everyone").

Apple Watch

  • VoiceOver · Larger Text · Dark Interface · Differentiate Without Color Alone · Sufficient Contrast · Reduced Motion

In progress

VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Reduced Motion and contrast on iPhone and iPad have not been verified on every screen yet, so they are not declared. We will add them as we fix them. Please tell us what gets in your way: mkingg04@gmail.com.
